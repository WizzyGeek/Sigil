How SIGIL Works

Your Identity

Your Stake

A registry signs your public key, issuing a short-lived stake — proof your key was valid at a specific moment in time.

Your Note

Your note and stake are wrapped into a Unified Note Token — a self-contained JWT. Anyone can verify it without a server.

04

The Network

Brokers fan your note out to listeners. Every client independently verifies the proof. Brokers can't forge — the math won't allow it.

"Unlike traditional platforms, SIGIL servers are cryptographically incapable of lying about authorship. Your note either verifies — or it doesn't."

Why this matters

You own your identity

Your identity is a keypair you generate. No account. No email required. No company can delete you, ban you, or hand your data to anyone. If you hold the key, you are you.

Notes are unforgeable

Every note carries a cryptographic signature. Tamper with one byte and the signature breaks. There is no moderation layer that can silently edit what you said.

The network has no owner

Anyone can run a relay broker. Anyone can run a registry. SIGIL is a protocol, not a platform. No company controls it. No single server can take it down.
